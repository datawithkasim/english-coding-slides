THINK LIKE A DEBATER
🧠 How to break an argument

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Could something else explain the same thing?
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Interrupting, legally
Raise a hand: Point of information. They answer Accepted or Not now.
Accepted gives you 15 seconds, and it must be a question. One per speech.
The three that always work: "Says who?" · "So what?" · "Always?"

Never the person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

For 찬성

Reasons to say YES

  • Never runs out of patience
  • Goes at your speed
  • Available at any hour
  • It can be paused and replayed
  • It reaches students with no school nearby

Two more nobody expects:

A recorded lesson can be paused. A teacher cannot.
Use it: Play it when they say one speed cannot fit everyone.
Millions learn online because there is no school near them.
Use it: Move the debate from best-case to no-teacher-at-all.
Now argue
