ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

AGAINST · ARGUMENTS
Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • A teacher sees your face
  • Learning needs encouragement
  • It can be confidently wrong
  • It cannot tell you are having a bad day
  • You learn to disagree with people, not screens
Discuss

Has a teacher ever noticed something about you that you never said?

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
A recorded lesson can be paused. A teacher cannot.
Use it: Play it when they say one speed cannot fit everyone.
FOR
Millions learn online because there is no school near them.
Use it: Move the debate from best-case to no-teacher-at-all.
AGAINST
A teacher notices you are quiet today and asks why.
Use it: Ask them which sensor does that.
AGAINST
A class is where you practise disagreeing with a person.
Use it: Say the subject is not the only subject.

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
