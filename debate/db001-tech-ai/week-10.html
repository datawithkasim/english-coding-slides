ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

FOR · ARGUMENTS
For 찬성

Reasons to say YES

  • Same rule for everyone, every time
  • Cannot be charmed or tired
  • Its rule can be checked
  • A rule cannot be charmed or hungry before lunch
  • You can read the rule and check it afterwards
Discuss

Which of these is biggest? Use the test: how many, how bad, can you undo it.

AGAINST · ARGUMENTS
Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • Learns old unfairness, at scale
  • Fair is not always identical
  • You cannot argue with it
  • One unfair person harms a few. One unfair rule harms all.
  • It learned from decisions people already made
Discuss

Name a rule at your school that is the same for everyone and still unfair.

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
A rule cannot be charmed, tired, or hungry before lunch.
Use it: Name the human faults the rule removes.
FOR
You can read a rule. You cannot read a person's mind.
Use it: Fairness you can check beats fairness you must trust.
AGAINST
It learned from decisions people already made.
Use it: Old unfairness, copied and speeded up.
AGAINST
One unfair person harms a few. One unfair rule harms everyone, every time.
Use it: That is weighing. Save it for your closing.

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.

Where they meet
