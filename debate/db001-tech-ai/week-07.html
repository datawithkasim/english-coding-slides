SKILL · 8 MIN
Today's skill

The four-step refutation

THEY SAY

Their argument, said fairly.

They say AI art takes five seconds, so it is not real art.

BUT

Where it fails.

But speed never decided what counts as art.

BECAUSE

The reason it fails.

Because a photograph takes a hundredth of a second.

THEREFORE

Why that wins the point.

Therefore their reason would rule out photography too.

Every part, every time. Miss one and it is not an argument yet.

SKILL · WATCH
⚠️ Watch

The usual mistake

Not this

"You don't understand art."

Attack the reason, never the person. That loses the round.

ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

FOR · ARGUMENTS
For 찬성

Reasons to say YES

  • A person still chooses

  • The prompt, the choosing and the editing are human
  • We already accept cameras and synthesisers as art tools

Take one of these through all four steps. Count them on your fingers.

AGAINST · ARGUMENTS
Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • No feeling, no expression
  • Built from artists' work, unasked
  • The struggle is part of the art
  • You cannot ask it why it chose that
  • It was trained on artists’ work without asking
Discuss

Would you feel differently about a picture if you were told after?

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
Photography, electric guitars and sampling were all called cheating.
Use it: Ask which of those they would still call fake.
FOR
A person still picks the idea, the words, and which one to keep.
Use it: Move the debate from who made it to who chose it.
AGAINST
The training used artists' work and nobody asked them.
Use it: This is about permission, not about quality.
AGAINST
You cannot ask it why it chose that.
Use it: If there is no reason behind a choice, was it a choice?

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
