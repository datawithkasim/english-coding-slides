🗣️SPEAKING
SKILL
Today's skill

The four-step refutation

THEY SAY

Their argument, said fairly.

They say AI art takes five seconds, so it is not real art.

BUT

Where it fails.

But speed never decided what counts as art.

BECAUSE

The reason it fails.

Because a photograph takes a hundredth of a second.

THEREFORE

Why that wins the point.

Therefore their reason would rule out photography too.

Every part, every time. Miss one and it is not an argument yet.

SAY IT LIKE THIS
Structure

The four-step refutation

The frame
They say . But . Because . Therefore .
They say AI art takes five seconds, so it is not real art. But speed never decided what counts as art. Because a photograph takes a hundredth of a second. Therefore their reason would rule out photography too.
Not this

"You don't understand art."

Attack the reason, never the person. That loses the round.

THINK LIKE A DEBATER
🧠 How to break an argument

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Could something else explain the same thing?
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Interrupting, legally
Raise a hand: Point of information. They answer Accepted or Not now.
Accepted gives you 15 seconds, and it must be a question. One per speech.
The three that always work: "Says who?" · "So what?" · "Always?"

Never the person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.
