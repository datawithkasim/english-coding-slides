SKILL · WATCH
⚠️ Watch

The usual mistake

Not this

"…and also there's another thing, um, so yeah, that's it."

A closing has no new arguments. Remind · weigh · stop.

ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

Reasons to say YES

AGAINST · ARGUMENTS
Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • Saying it hurts is not feeling it
  • Rights come with duties
  • Protection taken from people and animals
  • Saying that hurts is a sentence, not proof
  • We would be guessing, and guessing is not a right
Discuss

Which single one would you keep if you could only keep one?

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
We protect animals because they can suffer, not because they can argue.
Use it: Suffering is a test we already use.
FOR
If we are wrong, we caused real pain and called it nothing.
Use it: Weigh the two mistakes. One is much worse.
AGAINST
Saying 'that hurts' is a sentence. It is not proof of pain.
Use it: Ask for the test. No test, no case.
AGAINST
Rights come with duties. Name one duty a machine can carry.
Use it: A question they cannot answer is worth two arguments.

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
