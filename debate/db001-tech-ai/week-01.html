AI and Homework

This week's motion
Students should be allowed to use AI to help with homework.

SLOT 2
How this works

Four rules

  • Sides are drawn at random. You may get one you disagree with.
  • That is the point — you cannot beat an argument you do not understand.
  • Turns are short. 30 to 45 seconds. Nobody talks for five minutes.
  • Attack the reason, never the person.
