오늘 순서

RULES
How this works

Four rules

  • Sides are drawn at random. You may get one you disagree with.
  • That is the point — you cannot beat an argument you do not understand.
  • Turns are short. 30 to 45 seconds. Nobody talks for five minutes.
  • Attack the reason, never the person.

SKILL · WATCH
⚠️ Watch

The usual mistake

Not this

"I think AI homework help is good."

No because. An opinion with no reason does not count.

ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
Calculators were banned in schools, then allowed.
Use it: Ask them where the line is. Every tool was cheating first.
FOR
A student with a private tutor already gets help at home.
Use it: Turn it into fairness: AI is the tutor for everyone else.
AGAINST
There is no AI in the exam hall.
Use it: If the exam is the real test, homework has to build for it.
AGAINST
Homework exists to show the teacher what you cannot do yet.
Use it: AI hides the gap. Nobody fixes what nobody can see.

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
