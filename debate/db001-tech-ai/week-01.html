SAY IT LIKE THIS
Structure

Claim + reason

The frame
I think because .
I think winter is bad because I hate being cold.
Not this

"I think AI homework help is good."

No because. An opinion with no reason does not count.

THINK LIKE A DEBATER
🧠 How to break an argument

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Could something else explain the same thing?
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Interrupting, legally
Raise a hand: Point of information. They answer Accepted or Not now.
Accepted gives you 15 seconds, and it must be a question. One per speech.
The three that always work: "Says who?" · "So what?" · "Always?"

Never the person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

Reasons to say YES

Reasons to say NO

  • Unfair to students without it
  • The exam hall has no AI in it
  • You stop struggling, and struggling is the learning

Where they meet

The two lines that decide it

They say — "It is a tool, like a calculator"
Answer: A calculator does the arithmetic. It does not write your ideas.
They say — "You learn by reading the answer"
Answer: Reading an answer is not the same as finding one.
Argue it

Take one. Do you believe the answer, or can you beat it?

LAST QUESTIONS
Before you go

Answer with a reason

If a tool does the boring part, is the rest still your work?
Then ask: Name the boring part. Now name the part nobody can do for you.
A classmate gets top marks with AI. Name the feeling, then name the reason for it.
Then ask: Is that unfair, or only annoying? They are different arguments.
No teacher can tell who used AI. Does that change whether it is wrong?
Then ask: Does being caught make a thing wrong, or only make it risky?
