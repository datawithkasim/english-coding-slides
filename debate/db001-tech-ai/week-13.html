SKILL · WATCH
⚠️ Watch

The usual mistake

Not this

"A friend is a buddy."

That is a synonym, not a definition. A definition gives you a test.

ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

AGAINST · ARGUMENTS
Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • It cannot choose you
  • It cannot be hurt, so nothing is at risk
  • Replaces the practice of friendship
  • Switch it off and nothing is lost on its side
  • The company can change it or start charging for it
Discuss

Attack the definition, not the reason. What is too wide about it?

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
It is there at 3am, and a friend is asleep.
Use it: Do not claim it is better. Claim it is there.
FOR
People already feel real things about characters in books.
Use it: Answers 'it is not a real person' using something they accept.
AGAINST
It cannot leave you, so it never chose to stay.
Use it: Choosing is the test. Put it in your definition.
AGAINST
The company can change it, charge for it, or switch it off.
Use it: Ask who the friendship really belongs to.

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
