ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

FOR · ARGUMENTS
For 찬성

Reasons to say YES

  • Never drinks, texts or sleeps
  • Sensors watch every direction
  • Reacts faster than a foot moves
  • It sees in the dark and behind itself at once
  • Most crashes start with a tired or distracted driver

AGAINST · ARGUMENTS
Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • Only knows what it was built for
  • Nobody knows who is responsible
  • A machine follows a given rule
  • A driver can be blamed. A software update cannot.
  • The road keeps inventing situations nobody trained it on
Discuss

If a driverless car hits someone, who do you take to court?

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
Most crashes start with someone tired, drunk or on a phone.
Use it: Compare it with a real driver, not a perfect one.
FOR
A car can watch the dark, the sides and behind at the same time.
Use it: Play it when they say a person sees more.
AGAINST
A driver can be blamed. A software update cannot.
Use it: Ask who goes to court.
AGAINST
The road keeps inventing situations nobody trained it for.
Use it: Answers 'it reacts faster'. Fast at the wrong thing is worse.

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
