Not this

Stating their point weakly so it is easy to beat.

Everyone notices. Say it strongly, then beat it.

THINK LIKE A DEBATER
🧠 How to break an argument

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Could something else explain the same thing?
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Interrupting, legally
Raise a hand: Point of information. They answer Accepted or Not now.
Accepted gives you 15 seconds, and it must be a question. One per speech.
The three that always work: "Says who?" · "So what?" · "Always?"

Never the person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

Against 반대

Reasons to say NO

  • Only knows what it was built for
  • Nobody knows who is responsible
  • A machine follows a given rule

Two more nobody expects:

A driver can be blamed. A software update cannot.
Use it: Ask who goes to court.
The road keeps inventing situations nobody trained it for.
Use it: Answers 'it reacts faster'. Fast at the wrong thing is worse.

CLASH
Where they meet

The two lines that decide it

They say — "It never gets tired"
Answer: Tired is not the only way to crash.
They say — "It reacts faster"
Answer: Reacting fast to the wrong thing is worse than slowly to the right thing.
Argue it

Take one. Do you believe the answer, or can you beat it?
