추가 근거 · 하나 골라서 쓰세요

CLASH
Where they meet

The two lines that decide it

They say — "Shy students will ask it"
Answer: So we fix shyness by removing the need to talk to people?
They say — "It saves teacher time"
Answer: Only if the teacher trusts it.
Argue it

Take one. Do you believe the answer, or can you beat it?

ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

말하는 중에 손 들고 질문해요 · 15초

PREP · 11 MIN
Build your case

Four things, in this order

  • Argument one — claim, reason, example, and why it matters.
  • Argument two — a different reason. Not the same one again.
  • Their best argument — guess it, write it down, and plan the answer.
  • Two points of information — written as questions, ready to stand up with.
Timekeeper

Call out the time at 6 minutes and at 2 minutes left.

REBUTTAL
Answer them

Pick one point and kill it

Four steps, in order
They say .
But .
Because .
Therefore .

Aim at the reason, the example or the link. Never the person.

No new arguments. Stopping at Because is the commonest fault in this class.

CLOSING · 30s
Finish

Remind · weigh · stop

Closing frame
Today we showed and .
The most important is , because .
For that reason, you should vote for our side.
30

A closing has no new arguments in it.

VOTE · 12 MIN
Judging

Vote on the arguments

  • Not on the side you agree with. On who argued better.
  • Which point was never answered?
  • Who reached Therefore, and who stopped early?
  • Whose point of information did the most damage?
Reflection

One sentence: the best thing the other side said today.
