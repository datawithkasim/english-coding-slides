SKILL · WATCH
⚠️ Watch

The usual mistake

Not this

"If you did nothing wrong, why worry?"

The strongest line in the debate. Answer: I close the bathroom door and I am doing nothing wrong.

ATTACK · HOW
Break it

Three places to attack

1 · The reason
Is that actually why? Ask whether something else explains the same thing.
2 · The example
Is one example enough? Does it even show the thing they said?
3 · The link
Strongest attack: even if that is true, it does not prove the motion.
Never

The person. "You do not understand" loses the round on its own.

이유 · 예시 · 연결 고리, 이 세 곳을 공격해요

POI · THE MOVE
Interrupting, legally

"Point of information!"

How it works
1 · They are speaking. Raise a hand and say Point of information.
2 · The speaker answers Accepted or Not now. Both are allowed.
3 · Accepted, and you get 15 seconds. It must be a question, not a speech.
4 · One per speech. Two is rude, and the judge notices.
The three that always work

"Says who?"  ·  "So what?"  ·  "Always?"

Reasons to say NO

  • Children learn being watched is normal
  • Face data can leak or be sold
  • Misreads some faces more than others
  • You can change a password. You cannot change your face.
  • Being watched changes people who did nothing wrong
Discuss

Who in this debate was never asked what they wanted?

POINTS OF INFORMATION
Extra ammo

Four more things you can say

FOR
A stranger walking in is spotted before they reach a classroom.
Use it: Make the danger specific, or it is only a feeling.
FOR
The camera does not gossip about who was late.
Use it: A machine forgets. A staff room does not.
AGAINST
You can change a password. You cannot change your face.
Use it: One line, whole argument. Learn it.
AGAINST
Being watched changes what people do, even when they did nothing wrong.
Use it: Answers 'if you did nothing wrong, why worry?'

None of these are in the two lists. Steal one.
