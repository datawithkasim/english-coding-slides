SLOT 6
Run it

Top 10 of 2019

import requests
from bs4 import BeautifulSoup

year = input("Year (2000-2025)? ")
url  = f"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-pop_chart_{year}"
soup = BeautifulSoup(requests.get(url).text, "html.parser")

songs = [tr.get_text(strip=True) for tr in soup.select("table.wikitable tr")[1:11]]
for i, s in enumerate(songs, 1):
    print(f"{i}. {s}")
▸ Output
1. BTS — Boy with Luv
2. IZ*ONE — La Vie en Rose…
