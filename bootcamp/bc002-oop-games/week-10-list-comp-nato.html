SLOT 9
⚠️ Watch out

Key vs value

✗ broken 
{v: k for k, v in nato.items()}

Flipped — "Kilo" → "K" (reverse).

✓ fixed 
{k: v for k, v in nato.items()}

Letter → word as expected.

SLOT 12
🐛 Debugactivity

Generator, not list

result = (x*2 for x in nums)
print(result)
<generator object <genexpr> at 0x...>
Reveal fix

Use square brackets for a list: [x*2 for x in nums] or wrap with list().

() 는 제너레이터, [] 가 리스트
