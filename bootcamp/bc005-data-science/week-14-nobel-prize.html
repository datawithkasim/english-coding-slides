⚠️ Watch out

NaN hides facts

✗ broken 
df.groupby("country")\
  ["age"].mean()

Some ages are NaN — silently skipped.

✓ fixed 
df = df.dropna(subset=["age"])
df.groupby("country")["age"].mean()

Decide before grouping.

SLOT 10
🐛 Debugactivity

Find the bug — wrong agg

# "how many prizes per country"
df.groupby("country")["year"].sum()
Got sum of year values (huge numbers)
Reveal fix

"How many" = .count() or .size(), not sum.

개수는 count, 합계는 sum

Find the bug — missing data

df["birth_country"].value_counts().head()

value_counts drops NaN by default. Add dropna=False to include them.
