SLOT 9
⚠️ Watch out

Off-by-one index

✗ broken 
moves = ["r", "p", "s"]
print(moves[3])

→ IndexError

✓ fixed 
moves = ["r", "p", "s"]
print(moves[2])

→ "s" — last index is len−1
