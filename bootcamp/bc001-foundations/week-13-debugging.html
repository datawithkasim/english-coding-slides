SLOT 11
🐛 Debugactivity

Index out of range

cards = ["A", "K", "Q"]
for i in range(4):
    print(cards[i])
IndexError: list index out of range
Reveal fix

Use range(len(cards)) — never hardcode the size.

range는 len()으로 맞추기 — 크기 하드코딩 X

🐛 Debugactivity

Divide by zero

total = 100
people = 0

Reveal fix

Guard first: if people > 0: each = total / people.
