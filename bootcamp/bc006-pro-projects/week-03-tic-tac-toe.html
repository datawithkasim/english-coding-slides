Wrong axis

for i in range(3):

        print("col win")
Says "col win" but the line is actually a ROW
Reveal fix

For columns swap indexes: board[0][i] == board[1][i] == board[2][i].

행 검사: board[i][0..2], 열 검사: board[0..2][i]
