print("Saved!")

SLOT 11
🐛 Debugactivity

Transparency ignored

img  = Image.open("photo.jpg")
draw.text((10,10), "hi",
          fill=(255,255,255,120))
Text shows as solid white — alpha (120) ignored
Reveal fix

JPG is RGB only. Convert first: .convert("RGBA") to enable transparency.

JPG는 RGB — 투명도 쓰려면 RGBA로 변환

SLOT 12
🐛 Debugactivity

Save format wrong

img = img.convert("RGBA")
img.save("out.jpg")
OSError: cannot write mode RGBA as JPEG
Reveal fix

JPG can't hold alpha. Either use .png, or .convert("RGB") before save.

RGBA는 JPG로 못 저장 — PNG 사용
