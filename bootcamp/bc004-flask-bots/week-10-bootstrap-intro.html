⚠️ Watch out

col without row

✗ broken 
<div class="container">
  <div class="col-6">Hi</div>
</div>

→ ugly margins, broken grid

✓ fixed 
<div class="container">
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col-6">Hi</div>
  </div>
</div>

→ row wraps cols

SLOT 10
🐛 Debugactivity

Forgot meta viewport

<head>
  <link href="...bootstrap.css">
</head>
Page zooms out tiny on phones
Reveal fix

Add <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,initial-scale=1">.

반응형은 viewport meta 필수

SLOT 11
🐛 Debugactivity

container vs container-fluid

<div class="container-fluid">...
Content fills 100% of screen, not centered
Reveal fix

Use container for centered max-width. -fluid = always 100%.

container 가운데, -fluid 꽉 채움

SLOT 12
🐛 Debugactivity

Breakpoint confusion

<div class="col-12 col-lg-6">
Always full width — never splits to half
Reveal fix

Resize browser to 992px+ (lg). Or use col-md-6 for 768px.

분기점에 따라 다른 너비
