SLOT 6
Where numbers start 🧍

You are standing on 0

Every number in pos() is counted from where you stand.

Z
2 ⬜⬜⬜
1 ⬜⬜⬜
0 🧍⬛⬛
  0 1 2 X

X 0 and Z 0 are the lines you are standing on, so the grid starts at 1.

Y 0 is different. Y 0 is the floor at your feet, and blocks belong there.

Flat art sits at z 1, not z 0. First free square: pos(1, 0, 1).

내가 선 줄이 0이에요. X와 Z는 1부터, Y는 0부터예요.
