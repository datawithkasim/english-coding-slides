SLOT 7
Why 🧍

You are standing on 0

Every number in pos() is counted from where you stand.

Z
2 ⬜⬜⬜
1 ⬜⬜⬜
0 🧍⬛⬛
  0 1 2 X

X 0 and Z 0 are the lines you are standing on. Build there and you build on yourself, so the grid starts at 1.

Y 0 is different. Y 0 is the floor at your feet, and that is where blocks belong.

First free square: pos(1, 0, 1).

내가 서 있는 줄이 0이에요. 그래서 X, Z는 1부터. Y 0은 발밑 바닥이에요.

⚠️ Watch out

Common mistake

Lies flat. 6 wide, 3 tall.

✓ fixed 
pos(2, 0, 1), pos(4, 5, 1)

Stands up. 3 wide, 6 tall.

X first, then Y. Always.

X와 Y를 바꾸면 벽이 누워요.
