SLOT 16
⚠️ Watch out

Common mistake

You want a wall 3 wide and 6 tall.

✗ broken 
pos(0, 1, 0), pos(5, 3, 0)

Lies flat. 6 wide, 3 tall.

✓ fixed 
pos(1, 0, 0), pos(3, 5, 0)

Stands up. 3 wide, 6 tall.

X first, then Y. Always.

X와 Y를 바꾸면 벽이 누워요.
