SLOT 11
⚠️ Watch out

Degrees are not radians

✗ broken 
walk_x += math.cos(heading) * gap

→ cos(90) as radians ≈ −0.45. The square melts into a scribble.

✓ fixed 
walk_x += math.cos(
    math.radians(heading)) * gap

→ radians(90) first — cos gives 0, a clean corner.

cos/sin은 라디안만 먹어요
