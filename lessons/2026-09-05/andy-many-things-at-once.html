Mistake — .shift inside self.play

✗ broken

→ TypeError: ... cannot be converted to an animation

→ it slides

.shift() hands back a square. .animate.shift() hands back an animation.

Read back

Your whole scene

class MotionScene(Scene):
    def construct(self):
        circle = Circle(color=BLUE).shift(LEFT * 4)
        square = Square(color=YELLOW)
        title = Text("Motion").shift(RIGHT * 4)
        self.play(Create(circle), Create(square), Write(title))
        self.wait(1)
        group = VGroup(circle, square, title)
        self.play(group.animate.shift(UP * 2), run_time=2)
        self.wait(1)

Two self.play lines. Four animations. Five seconds of video.
