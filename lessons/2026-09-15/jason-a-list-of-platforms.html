Concept

Tap = small hop. Hold = big jump.

Right now every jump is identical. Real platformers let you choose.

The trick: when you let go early and you are still rising, cut vy. The arc ends sooner.

Nothing about gravity changes. You just interrupt the rise.

Concept

Coyote time 🐺

Run off a ledge, press jump a hair too late, nothing happens. Feels broken. It is not.

Real games keep letting you jump for about 6 frames after you leave the ground. Players never notice. They just think the game feels good.

A counter that ticks down, one every frame.

on ledge   coyote = 6
step off   coyote = 5  → jump OK
           coyote = 4  → jump OK
           coyote = 3  → jump OK
           ...
           coyote = 0  → too late
