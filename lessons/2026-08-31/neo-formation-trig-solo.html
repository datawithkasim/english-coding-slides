SLOT 19
⚠️ Watch out

cos is a ratio, not a distance

✗ broken 
dx = math.cos(angle)
dy = math.sin(angle)

cos never passes 1. Every ship sits within one pixel — the ring is a dot.

✓ fixed 
dx = math.cos(angle) * radius
dy = math.sin(angle) * radius

radius turns the ratio into pixels.

cos는 비율, radius가 크기

# 1. in the formation dict, under "pattern": pattern,
"spin": 0,

# 2. in the update loop, after  f["anchor_y"] += f["vy"]
f["spin"] += 0.03
for i, m in enumerate(f["members"]):
    a = (i / len(f["members"])) * 2 * math.pi + f["spin"]
    m["dx"] = math.cos(a) * 90
    m["dy"] = math.sin(a) * 90

Same formula, new angle every frame. The ring rotates.
