SLOT 9
⚠️ Watch out

Where do " and ) go?

✗ broken 
name = input("Name? "
print(f"Hi {name})"

a bracket is missing, a quote is late

✓ fixed 
name = input("Name? ")
print(f"Hi {name}")

every ( gets ), every " gets "

SLOT 18+
⚠ Edge cases OPTIONAL

Four gotchas

input() gives text
Type 7 and you get "7", not 7.
.strip() cuts ends only
Middle spaces stay: "big sword".
.title() hits every word
"iron sword" becomes "Iron Sword".
"=" * 0
Zero copies. You get an empty line.
