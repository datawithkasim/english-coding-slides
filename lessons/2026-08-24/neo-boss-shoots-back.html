SLOT 9
⚠️ Watch out

Capital R or no box

✗ broken 
hit_box = pygame.rect(
    b["x"], b["y"], 8, 16)

→ AttributeError: module 'pygame' has no attribute 'rect'

✓ fixed 
hit_box = pygame.Rect(
    b["x"], b["y"], 8, 16)

→ a real box you can test

pygame.Rect makes a box. pygame.draw.rect paints one. Different word, different job.

Rect는 상자, draw.rect는 그리기

if boss["shoot_cd"] <= 0:

    dy = player.y - boss["y"]
    dist = (dx * dx + dy * dy) ** 0.5
