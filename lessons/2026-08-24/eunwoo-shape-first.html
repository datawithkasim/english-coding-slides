SLOT 10
⚠️ Watch out

Common mistake: one fill per row

✗ 14 lines 
blocks.fill(BLACK_CONCRETE,
  pos(0,7,0), pos(6,7,0), REPLACE)
blocks.fill(BLACK_CONCRETE,
  pos(0,6,0), pos(6,6,0), REPLACE)
# … 12 more rows …

14 ranges to type. 14 chances to miscount.

✓ 4 lines 
blocks.fill(BLACK_CONCRETE,
  pos(0,0,0), pos(6,7,0), REPLACE)
# then 3 detail fills

1 range for the head. 3 for the details.

SLOT 26
MirrorOPTIONAL

Mirroring swaps start and end

# art is 8 wide, so the mirror line is 7 − x
new_start = 7 − old_end
new_end   = 7 − old_start

# left  row  pos(0,3,0) → pos(3,3,0)
# right row  pos(4,3,0) → pos(7,3,0)

7 − 3 = 4 becomes the start. The bigger number always goes second.
