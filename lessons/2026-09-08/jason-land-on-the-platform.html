Concept

Landing asks four questions at once

1  his right edge > its left edge
2  his left edge  < its right edge
3  his y >= its y
4  his y < its y + 20

All four true, he stands. One false, he falls straight past.

네 개가 전부 맞아야 착지해요

Test 1 of 4

Has he reached the left edge?

player["x"] + player["w"] > plat["x"]

His right edge is his x plus his width. It must be past the platform's left edge.

Test 2 of 4

Has he gone past the right edge?

player["x"] < plat["x"] + plat["w"]

The platform's right edge is its x plus its width. His left edge must still be before it.

Test 3 of 4

Has he dropped to the top of it?

player["y"] >= plat["y"]

Same shape as your ground check, player["y"] >= GROUND_Y. Only the number on the right changed.

Test 4 of 4

Is he only just past it?

player["y"] < plat["y"] + 20

Without this, anyone below the platform gets yanked up onto it. 20 pixels is the catching window.

Concept

Coyote time 🐺

Run off a ledge, press jump a hair too late, nothing happens. Feels broken. It is not.

Real games keep letting you jump for about 6 frames after you leave the ground. Players never notice. They just think the game feels good.

A counter that ticks down, one every frame.

on ledge   coyote = 6
step off   coyote = 5  → jump OK
           coyote = 4  → jump OK
           coyote = 3  → jump OK
           ...
           coyote = 0  → too late

발판에서 떨어진 직후 몇 프레임은 아직 점프할 수 있어요.
