Big picture

Most walls are grey cobblestone. But at every corner there is one coloured block.

The agent walks straight until something blocks it. Then it looks at the colour and obeys.

The rules

Five signals, five actions

GOLD_BLOCKturn LEFT EMERALD_BLOCKturn RIGHT REDSTONE_BLOCKa door — break it, then walk through LAPIS_LAZULI_BLOCKa low wall — hop up, over, down DIAMOND_BLOCKthe GOAL — stop

Anything else (plain cobblestone) → turn right and try again.

금=왼쪽, 에메랄드=오른쪽, 레드스톤=문, 청금석=넘기, 다이아=골인

Why it lags

📡 Every question is a trip

if and elif are free. agent.inspect() is not — it stops your code and waits for Minecraft to answer.

calling agent.inspect() inside every elif126 questions ≈ 25 s
reading it once into a variable34 questions ≈ 7 s

Same maze. Same 28 loop turns. Same answer, 3.7× faster.

질문 한 번에 게임을 한 번 기다려요. 질문을 줄이면 빨라져요
