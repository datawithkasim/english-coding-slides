Why plan first?

  • Spot problems before typing
  • Real designers always sketch
  • Code becomes faster & cleaner

3 questions to answer

  • Who's it for? (friends? teachers? recruiters?)
  • What 3 words describe you?
  • What 3 projects to show?

Write the answers down.

3가지 질문에 답하기

Pre-design

Pick palette + font

  • 2 main colors + 1 accent
  • 1 font (Google Fonts has free ones)
  • Spacing scale: 8 / 16 / 24 / 32

Decide before coding. Less indecision later.
