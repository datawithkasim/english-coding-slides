Battle logic

Compare stats with if/elif/else

my_atk = pikachu["attack"]
enemy_hp = enemy["hp"]

if my_atk > enemy_hp:
    print("💥 You win!")
elif my_atk == enemy_hp:
    print("🤝 Tie!")
else:
    print("😵 You lost...")

Random battle

import random

all_pokemon = [pikachu, charmander, squirtle]

my_choice = pikachu
enemy = random.choice(all_pokemon)

print(f"You sent out {my_choice['name']}!")
print(f"A wild {enemy['name']} appears!")
Bonus

Random damage too

damage = random.randint(10, 30)
print(f"You hit for {damage} damage!")

randint(a, b) — random number from a to b.

⚠️ Common bug
