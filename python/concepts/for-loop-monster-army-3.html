Words

Three new words

outer loop the loop on the outside — it counts the waves 바깥쪽 반복문 inner loop the loop inside — it runs fully every time 안쪽 반복문 nested one thing sitting inside another thing 중첩
Idea 💡

Two workers, not one

Outer worker shouts "WAVE 1!"

Inner worker sends every monster.

Then outer shouts "WAVE 2!"

Outer moves once. Inner runs all the way.

바깥 1번 = 안쪽 전부 1바퀴

Careful ⚠

The inner loop must be indented

Broken 
for wave in range(1, 4):
for monster in monsters:
    print(monster * wave)

IndentationError. The outer loop has nothing inside it, so Python stops before running.

Fixed 
for wave in range(1, 4):
    for monster in monsters:
        print(monster * wave)

4 spaces, then 8. Inside means indented.
