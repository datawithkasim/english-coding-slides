SLOT 8
⚠️ Watch out

Mixed up and / or

✗ wrong 
if "map" in bag or "sword" in bag:
    print("Hero ending")

"or" → only ONE needed → too easy

✓ right 
if "map" in bag and "sword" in bag:
    print("Hero ending")

"and" → BOTH required
