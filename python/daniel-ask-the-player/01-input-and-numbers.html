02 WATCH IT f-string

The f opens a slot

you wroteYou have {money} dollars in your bank!
the boxmoney = '100'

No f, no slot. You would see {money} on screen.

05 WHY quotes

Quotes change everything

str · text
'100'
  • '100' + '5' → '1005'
  • '100' - 20 → breaks
int · number
100
  • 100 + 5 → 105
  • 100 - 20 → 80

Text gets glued. Numbers get counted.
